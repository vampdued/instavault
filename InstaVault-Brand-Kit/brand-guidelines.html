InstaVault

Brand guidelines. One camera, one dial, one V: the simplest way to say your moments are safe.

01 · Logo

A camera with a vault inside

The icon is a familiar camera glyph. Its lens doubles as a vault dial, and the V at the center of the lens stands for Vault. It is a single shape, so it stays readable from a 16 px toolbar icon to a billboard.

InstaVault

Camera body

A rounded square with soft, consistent corners. Instantly a camera.

Dial lens

A bold white ring reads as a lens and as the dial of a vault door.

The V

A rounded chevron: Vault, and a sense of things moving safely inside.

Flash dot

The upper-right dot completes the camera and balances the composition.

05 · Typography

Inter, in two weights

The wordmark pairs Inter Medium for “Insta” with Inter ExtraBold for “Vault”, with slightly tight letter-spacing. Use Inter across product and marketing.

InstaVault
Headlines · Inter ExtraBold 800
Subheads · Inter SemiBold 600
Body copy · Inter Regular 400, 16/1.6. Short sentences, plain words, calm confidence.
